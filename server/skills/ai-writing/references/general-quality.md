# General quality rubric

- Lead with the reader's takeaway; organize for scanning before adding detail.
- Match the stated audience, purpose, channel, language, tone, and length.
- Prefer strong specific verbs, concrete nouns, short sentences, and consistent terminology.
- Use headings, parallel lists, tables, and steps only when they improve comprehension.
- Separate fact from assumption. Preserve names, numbers, constraints, and source meaning.
- Remove repetition, vague filler, keyword stuffing, exaggerated claims, and generic AI phrases.
- Make the next action clear when the format calls for one.
- Final review: accuracy, instruction coverage, structure, readability, platform fit, originality, and Markdown validity.
