# Competitor analysis

- Compare equivalent page types, search tasks, topics, headings, and visible content from user-supplied competitor URLs.
- A content gap means a competitor covers a relevant task that the target does not cover in the crawled sample. It does not prove traffic or ranking advantage.
- Keyword and SERP gaps require provider data. Keep discovered themes separate from measured keyword gaps.
- Prioritize gaps by audience fit, product relevance, business value, effort, and evidence strength.
- Avoid copying competitor wording. Convert insights into an original page brief.
